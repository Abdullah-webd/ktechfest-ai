The problem

6:40 PM. Amara is closing her shop.

A cousin heard something on WhatsApp. A neighbour saw movement on the road. The vigilante group knows what's going on, but their radio only reaches each other. By the time real news reaches Amara it's either too late, or it's a rumour that empties the whole market for nothing.

SafeRoad is the bridge from the radio circle to the town.

{{ icon('sparkles','w-3.5 h-3.5') }} The agent

Not a chatbot. An agent that acts.

Every message, typed or spoken, goes to the SafeRoad agent. It works out what the person means in their language, remembers what they asked before, checks the community's live record of responder reports, decides which tools to run, and then replies with text and a native voice. It never invents an answer: if no responder has reported something, it says so and goes to find out.

1

Understand

Transcribes voice notes in five languages, follows the conversation, resolves follow-ups like "any update?"

2

Verify

Matches the question against what responders have confirmed in the last 24 hours, and grades it: confirmed, contradicted, unconfirmed, or no information.

3

Act

Runs the right tool: publish an alert, translate it for every resident, wake up responders, file a report, or hand over an on-duty responder's number.

4

Speak

Replies in the person's language, in text and in a native Nigerian voice, and notifies everyone who needs to know.

Tools the agent can run

{{ icon('megaphone','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} post_alert

Turns a responder's words into a structured alert and broadcasts it to every resident in their own language.

{{ icon('check-circle','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} verify_rumour

Checks a message a resident received against the live responder record and grades it honestly.

{{ icon('help','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} escalate_to_responders

When nobody has reported it yet, forwards the question to responders and replies to the asker when one answers.

{{ icon('phone','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} connect_on_duty

Offers a tap-to-call button for the responder currently on duty, so nobody waits for an answer.

{{ icon('globe','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} translate

Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo, Pidgin and English, both ways, so a Hausa voice note becomes a Yorùbá alert.

{{ icon('zap','w-4 h-4 text-brand-600') }} all_clear

Closes an open alert when a responder says it's over, and updates everyone who asked about it.

Built on Nigerian-language speech models for voice in and out, and a reasoning model that plans and runs these tools.

How it works

Three steps, no training needed

{{ icon('mic','w-5 h-5') }}

Responders speak

A vigilante member sees something and taps the mic: "Fight near the market, avoid it." In any language. The agent turns it into a clear, structured alert and decides who needs to hear it.

{{ icon('bell','w-5 h-5') }}

Everyone hears, instantly

Every resident's phone buzzes, in their own language, even with the app closed. The alert lands in their chat with who said it and when.

{{ icon('chat','w-5 h-5') }}

Ask before you panic

Heard a rumour? Forward it to the agent. It answers only from responder reports, and if nobody knows yet it escalates to responders for you and gives you someone to call.

Why trust it

Four honest answers. Never a guess.

The agent is not allowed to say "safe" or "true" on its own. It reports what responders reported, names them, and says so when it doesn't know.

Confirmed

"Musa confirmed a fight near the market 12 minutes ago. Avoid Kaduna road."

Contradicted

"Musa reported the market clear 8 minutes ago. The message you received is not confirmed."

Unconfirmed

"3 residents reported this, no responder has confirmed. I've asked them and will message you."

No information

"No responder has reported anything about Kaduna road today. I've asked them. You can call Musa, who is on duty."
