How it works

🎤

Responders speak

A vigilante member sees something and taps the mic: "Fight near the market, avoid it." Any language. The AI turns it into a clear alert.

🔔

Everyone hears, instantly

Every resident's phone buzzes, in their own language, even with the app closed. Who said it and when is always shown.

🤖

Ask before you panic

Heard a rumour? Ask SafeRoad. It answers only from responder reports. If nobody knows yet, it asks them for you and gives you a responder to call.

Four honest answers, never a guess

The AI is not allowed to say "safe" or "true" on its own. Every answer names the responder and the time.

Confirmed

"Musa confirmed a fight near the market 12 minutes ago. Avoid Kaduna road."

Contradicted

"Musa reported the market clear 8 minutes ago. The message you received is not confirmed."

Unconfirmed

"3 residents reported this, no responder has confirmed. I've asked them and will notify you."

No information

"No responder has reported anything about Kaduna road today. I've asked them. You can call Musa, who is on duty."
