Add SafeRoad to your Home Screen

On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is on your Home Screen. It takes two taps:

  1. 1Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. 2Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. 3Open SafeRoad from your Home Screen and tap Enable alerts.
