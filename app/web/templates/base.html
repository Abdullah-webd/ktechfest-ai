Add SafeRoad to your Home Screen

On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is on your Home Screen. Three taps:

  1. 1Tap Share {{ icon('share','w-4 h-4 inline -mt-0.5 text-info-600') }} at the bottom of Safari.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen {{ icon('plus-square','w-4 h-4 inline -mt-0.5') }}, then Add.
  3. 3Open SafeRoad from your Home Screen and tap Turn on alerts.
